# Agent findings: the two 2023 amendments (researched 2026-10-21; applied to the note and the codebook on 2026-10-21 and 2026-10-22; kept for provenance)

Sources: Versa translation of Hayut's majority opinion and the official abstract in HCJ 5658/23; IDI; Times of Israel; Jerusalem Post; Kohelet. No Knesset-website vote record found for either amendment.

## A. Basic Law: the Government (Amendment No. 12), incapacity
- A1 Final vote: early 23 Mar 2023, 61 for / 47 against. JPost https://www.jpost.com/israel-news/politics-and-diplomacy/article-735156 ("passed early Thursday morning with 61 MKs voting in favor and 47 against"). Corroborated by ToI syndicated at https://www.australianjewishnews.com/knesset-passes-law-shielding-netanyahu-from-court-ordered-recusal-61-47/
- A2 Coalition-only: not stated explicitly for the final vote. First reading was party-line 61-51, ToI 13 Mar 2023 https://www.timesofisrael.com/knesset-advances-bill-protecting-pm-from-being-sidelined-over-conflicts-of-interest/ ("easily cleared its first vote 61-51 along party lines"). Absentees at final vote: Abbas (Ra'am), Likud MKs Amsalem and Bitan, Shas's Abutbul. 61 = 64 coalition minus 3 absent coalition members, but no source says so outright.
- A3 Immediate effect: yes, no deferral clause. IDI 3 Aug 2023 https://en.idi.org.il/articles/50405 ("Due to the personal nature and immediate enactment of the amendment"). Kohelet https://www.kohelet.org.il/en/?p=9571 (MKs "deliberately chose not to defer applicability to next Knesset").
- A4 First reading to final: 10 days (13 Mar to 23 Mar 2023). IDI https://en.idi.org.il/articles/50968 frames the whole process as about a month.
- A5 Litigation timing: MQG petition to declare Netanyahu unfit filed 9 Feb 2023, ToI https://timesofisrael.com/petition-asks-high-court-to-declare-netanyahu-unfit-for-office/ ; court set response deadline 12 Mar 2023, ToI https://www.timesofisrael.com/court-gives-netanyahu-1-month-to-respond-to-petition-to-declare-him-unfit-for-office/ ; but IDI (relaying the AG's filing) says the law passed "three days before the deadline for responding", i.e. about 26 Mar. CONFLICT unresolved; an extension is plausible but not found. Note: HCJ 2412/23 is the petition AGAINST the amendment (judgment 3 Jan 2024), not the incapacity petition. Medina fn. 35 cites 2412/23 as the Incapacity Case, consistent with this.
- A6 Judgment 3 Jan 2024: 6-5 to defer application to the next Knesset; not struck. ToI https://www.timesofisrael.com/liveblog_entry/high-court-delays-contentious-law-shielding-netanyahu-from-recusal/ ("rules six to five to postpone"). Majority Hayut, Vogelman, Amit, Barak-Erez, Baron, Grosskopf; minority Sohlberg, Mintz, Elron, Wilner, Stein. Court called it "clearly a personal amendment". JPost https://www.jpost.com/breaking-news/article-780627. Count of justices finding abuse of constituent power (as distinct from remedy): not found; no Versa translation.

## B. Basic Law: the Judiciary (Amendment No. 3), reasonableness
- B1 Tally 64-0, opposition boycott: confirmed. ToI https://www.timesofisrael.com/coalition-passes-1st-judicial-overhaul-law-limiting-review-of-government-decisions/ ; Hayut para. 26, Versa https://versa.cardozo.yu.edu/node/705 ("approved in a third reading by a majority of 64 members of Knesset without opposing votes, after the opposition factions boycotted the vote").
- B2 Immediate effect: in force on publication 26 Jul 2023. Hayut para. 26 ("The Amendment came into force on July 26, 2023, upon its publication in the Official Gazette"). Official abstract https://versa.cardozo.yu.edu/node/704 ("its immediate entry into force").
- B3 First reading to final: 13 to 14 days (first reading 10/11 Jul 2023, 64-56; final 24 Jul). Hayut para. 22-23: four committee debates over nine days plus three on objections; para. 194: "the legislative process took only about a month"; accelerated via the objections procedure and sec. 98 of the Knesset Rules; para. 195: participation threshold for intervention not crossed. ToI https://timesofisrael.com/bill-to-erase-judicial-reasonableness-test-for-politicians-passes-1st-reading/ and https://timesofisrael.com/rothman-plows-ahead-with-reasonableness-bill-despite-criticism-over-rapid-process/
- B4 Litigation timing: Deri ruling HCJ 8948/22 decided 18 Jan 2023; coalition vowed a reasonableness law the same day, ToI https://timesofisrael.com/in-bombshell-ruling-high-court-nixes-shas-head-deri-from-serving-as-minister/ . CAVEAT: Hayut para. 12 records Levin's 1 Jan 2023 speech already listed abolishing reasonableness, and Rothman's 17 Jan 2023 bill contained it, so the plan predates Deri; Deri was the political trigger in press accounts. Pending proceedings at enactment: yes, per the Knesset's own affidavit, Hayut para. 101 ("there are already pending proceedings that raise arguments concerning the reasonableness of decisions by the elected echelon"); AG pointed to pending HCJ 8987/22 (Police Ordinance Amendment 37, Ben-Gvir powers); petition against Ben-Gvir's appointment on extreme-unreasonableness grounds pending from Dec 2022, JPost https://www.jpost.com/israel-news/politics-and-diplomacy/article-732576 . No AG-specific pending reasonableness petition in mid-2023 found.
- B5 Judgment 1 Jan 2024, 15-justice bench: 8-7 to void; 12-3 that the Court may review Basic Laws (abstract, https://versa.cardozo.yu.edu/node/704). The 8: Hayut, Vogelman, Amit, Barak-Erez, Baron, Groskopf, Kabub, Ronen. Wilner, Stein, Canfy-Steinitz favoured narrow construction; Kasher refrained; Sohlberg, Mintz, Elron would dismiss. ToI counts 13-2 on authority (Elron's narrow exception counted), https://www.timesofisrael.com/in-historic-ruling-high-court-strikes-down-key-judicial-overhaul-legislation/ ; abstract's 12-3 is the better source.
