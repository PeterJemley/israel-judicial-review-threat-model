# Events after the article's 2025 cut-off (researched 2026-10-21, not yet applied to the note)

Working notes kept for provenance. Each line gives the event, the date, and the source read. Values are provisional until applied to the note with the change log entry.

- 2025-01 onward. Justice Minister Yariv Levin declined to convene the Judicial Selection Committee; by May 2026 the refusal had lasted sixteen months with 51 vacancies in the magistrate and district courts. Times of Israel, 31 May 2026: https://www.timesofisrael.com/high-court-orders-justice-minister-to-convene-judicial-selection-committee/
- 2025-03-27. Amendment to Basic Law: The Judiciary changing the Judicial Selection Committee passed 67 to 1; the opposition boycotted the final vote; the measure "will only come into effect in the next Knesset". Petitions were filed at once. Times of Israel: https://www.timesofisrael.com/knesset-passes-law-greatly-boosting-political-control-over-judicial-appointments/
- 2025-05-21. The High Court held the dismissal of the head of the Shin Bet unlawful and found the prime minister in a conflict of interest; the government had already rescinded the dismissal; no operative order; Deputy President Sohlberg dissented in part. Times of Israel: https://www.timesofisrael.com/high-court-rules-shin-bet-heads-dismissal-unlawful-pm-had-conflict-of-interest/
- 2025-08. The government voted to dismiss Attorney General Gali Baharav-Miara under a new procedure (Cabinet Resolution 3125); the High Court froze the dismissal. Times of Israel, 14 Dec 2025 (below).
- 2025-12-14. A seven-justice panel annulled the dismissal unanimously; the justice minister and the communications minister urged the government to disregard the ruling. Times of Israel: https://www.timesofisrael.com/high-court-annuls-firing-of-attorney-general-cabinet-ministers-urge-defiance-of-ruling/
- 2026-01-11. Overview of the renewed legislative package (attorney general, ministerial legal advisers, police internal investigations, military advocate general, a motion on Basic Law amendments). Times of Israel: https://www.timesofisrael.com/judicial-overhaul-redux-new-laws-aim-to-weaken-legal-checks-on-government-action/
- 2026-05-31. The High Court ordered the justice minister to convene the Judicial Selection Committee and publish candidates by 8 June; he called the ruling "an apparently illegal decision". Times of Israel, as above.
- 2026-06-21. All eleven serving justices heard the petitions against the Judicial Selection amendment. JNS: https://www.jns.org/news/israel-news/israeli-high-court-hears-petitions-against-law-reshaping-judicial-selection-committee . No ruling found by 2026-10-21.
- 2026-07-13. Basic Law: Torah Study passed 63 to 52, declaring Torah study "a foundational value in the heritage of the Jewish people and the State of Israel". JNS, 14 Jul 2026: https://www.jns.org/news/israel-news/knesset-passes-basic-law-declaring-torah-study-foundational-state-value
- 2026-07-15. The law ending the binding status of the attorney general's opinions passed its final readings; the Jerusalem Post reports 65 to 51 and Ynet reports 61 to 51 (unresolved); the split of the attorney general's role was dropped; effect 1 January 2027; petitions filed. Jerusalem Post: https://www.jpost.com/israel-news/politics-and-diplomacy/article-902638 ; Ynet: https://www.ynetnews.com/article/rk00rjbbefg
- 2026-07-17. The Knesset dissolved; election set for 27 October 2026. Library of Congress blog, 9 Oct 2026: https://blogs.loc.gov/law/2026/10/falqs-israels-upcoming-election-on-october-27-2026/ . The outgoing coalition held 60 of 120 seats. Al Jazeera, 20 Oct 2026: https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/20/israels-elections-2026-explained-in-maps-and-charts
- 2026-07 (early). At least ten petitions awaited rulings, including challenges to six laws; the government said it would not honour a ruling on the Second Authority council. Ynet: https://www.ynetnews.com/article/bkjg19yxfg
- 2026-07-27. The Knesset told the High Court that only severe damage to the country's democratic character could justify invalidating part of a Basic Law. Jerusalem Post: https://www.jpost.com/israel-news/politics-and-diplomacy/article-903801
- 2026-10-02. The Supreme Court unanimously reversed the Central Elections Committee's disqualification of Ra'am and the Joint List; the disqualification motions had come from right-wing groups including Likud and Otzma Yehudit. Times of Israel: https://www.timesofisrael.com/supreme-court-unanimously-overturns-election-panels-ban-on-arab-parties/

Observation for the change log, made after seeing these events and therefore exploratory: the 2025 and 2026 enactments were given deferred effect, which sidesteps the Court's "immediate effect" and "serves the current government" indicators. Base-rate caveat for the 2026-10-02 item: disqualification motions against Arab-majority lists have been brought and reversed in earlier election cycles as well.
