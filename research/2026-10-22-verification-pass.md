## Public sources (fetched outside Westlaw)

6. Versa translation of Agudat Derekh Eretz v. Broadcasting Authority (1981). Established: Landau P. states that the Ressler case [2] discussed the Confirmation Law and concluded that it "indeed ratified 'the election Laws' mentioned therein" by absolute majority, "but it says nothing with respect to the question whether the validity of the election Laws or any of their provisions" meet equality. The translation does not say which provision was challenged in Ressler. Panel: Landau P., Shamgar, Ben-Porat, Barak, Bejski; judgment 28 July 1981.
7. Hebrew Wikisource, Interpretation Law 5741-1981. Established: s. 3 defines חיקוק as a statute or regulation; s. 21 provides that an enactment's validity begins at 00:01 on its day of commencement; s. 32 and s. 33 are the law's own commencement and publication clauses. No section gives a default commencement day.
8. Hebrew Wikisource, Interpretation Ordinance [New Version]. Established: s. 17 provides that regulations with legislative effect take effect on the day of publication unless otherwise provided. Regulations only.
9. Hebrew Wikisource, Law and Administration Ordinance 5708-1948. Established: s. 10(a) provides that every ordinance takes effect on the day of its publication in Reshumot unless it provides for an earlier or later date. Written for ordinances.
10. Hebrew Wikisource, Transition Law 5709-1949. Established: s. 2(d) requires publication of every law in Reshumot within ten days of its passage; s. 2(c) is repealed; no section states a default commencement day for laws.
