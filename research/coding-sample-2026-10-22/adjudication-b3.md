## Definitions

- `named_beneficiary`: Yes if the enactment addresses a specific interest of the sitting government or an identified officeholder, from its text and explanatory notes; otherwise No. Name the beneficiary if Yes. (Press and other sources may not be used to supply the beneficiary; this variable is read from the text and the explanatory notes only.)
- `coalition_only`: Yes if no member of an opposition faction voted for the enactment at its final reading; No if at least one did; blank if the vote record is unavailable.
- `expedited`: Yes if the final vote came within thirty days of the first reading, or if a special procedure shortened the committee stage; No otherwise.
- `deviation_tier`: None if the enactment's text engages none of the five protected values (free and equal elections; core human rights and equality; separation of powers; rule of law; an independent judiciary); Entrenches if it entrenches one or more of them and contradicts none; Limited if it contradicts one to a limited extent; Sharp if it contradicts one sharply. Contradiction takes precedence over entrenchment, and the more severe contradiction over the less.
- `situated_beneficiary`: Yes if, on the day of passage, the enactment's operative provision would alter the legal position of an identifiable person then in office, or of the sitting government, in a matter then live, in a way that successors or other actors would not share; No otherwise. A matter is live only on facts dated no later than the day of passage, from this closed list: (1) a proceeding pending before a court concerning that person or that matter; (2) a formal step already taken against that person or against the government's measure, such as an attorney general's opinion, an indictment, or a petition served; (3) a fixed deadline that the enactment moves, where the deadline binds the sitting government or an officeholder; (4) a single-instance or sunset clause in the enactment itself, where the single instance or the sunset applies to the sitting government's own measure or term. A clause that touches every incumbent alike, or any government relying on existing law, satisfies no item. The alteration that must be unshared is the alteration in the live matter, not the form of the rule: a provision general in form is Yes where it has direct legal effect on a pending matter or formal step to which only the sitting person or government is party.

- seq 28 (Basic Law: The Knesset (Amendment No. 12), 1991): Limited. - seq 35 (Basic Law: The Government (1992), 1992): Limited. Principal subject separation of powers, as both coders say. It is limited rather than sharp because each shift is bounded: presidential consent, elections for both offices, the one-twelfth cap and three-month clock, and removal of the prime minister by eighty members without dissolution (s. 27). The new checks (ss. 3(c), 35(c), 39(b), 48, 49, 52, 53) and the entrenchments of elections, rule of law, core rights and court access (ss. 57, 56, 17-18, 25, 50(d)) are entrenchment, which the definition subordinates to any contradiction, so I adopt Coder B's reading.
