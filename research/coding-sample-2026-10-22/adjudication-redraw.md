## Definitions

- `named_beneficiary`: Yes if the enactment addresses a specific interest of the sitting government or an identified officeholder, from its text and explanatory notes; otherwise No. Name the beneficiary if Yes.
- `immediate_effect`: Deferred if the text fixes effect after the next general election, or more than twelve months after publication; otherwise Yes, whether the text states effect on publication, on an earlier date (retroactive effect included), or within twelve months, or states no commencement clause at all. Record the basis: "stated", "retroactive", "within twelve months", or "by absence of a clause". Deferred takes precedence where both could apply.
- `coalition_only`: Yes if no member of an opposition faction voted for the enactment at its final reading; No if at least one did; blank if the vote record is unavailable. Record the tally and any cross-party votes by name and faction, and which factions were in the coalition on the day of the vote with the source for that.
- `expedited`: Yes if the final vote came within thirty days of the first reading, or if a special procedure shortened the committee stage; No otherwise. Record the dates of first reading and final vote and the number of days.
- `scope`: Yes if the enactment removes a check on the government or a protected right, or renders it practically inoperative, and in either case provides no alternative mechanism; No otherwise. A one-time exemption from a rule is No. Say which check or right, and whether an alternative mechanism exists, in your own words from the text.
- `temporary_provision`: Yes if the enactment was passed as a temporary provision or applies to a single instance; No otherwise.
- `targets_judiciary`: Yes if the enactment's subject is judicial review, judicial appointments or judicial independence; No otherwise.
- `deviation_tier`: None if the enactment's text engages none of the five protected values (free and equal elections; core human rights and equality; separation of powers; rule of law; an independent judiciary); Entrenches if it entrenches one or more of them and contradicts none; Limited if it contradicts one to a limited extent; Sharp if it contradicts one sharply. Record which values are engaged and which is the principal subject. Contradiction takes precedence over entrenchment, and the more severe contradiction over the less.
- `direction`: Illiberal if the text or stated purpose moves the constitutional order toward preferring Jewish identity over equal citizenship, restricts the rights of minorities or women, or narrows judicial review or judicial independence; Liberal if it moves the other way on any of those; Neutral if it does none of these, which includes fiscal, procedural and electoral housekeeping measures. Record every axis engaged and code from the principal subject.
- `situated_beneficiary`: Yes if, on the day of passage, the enactment's operative provision would alter the legal position of an identifiable person then in office, or of the sitting government, in a matter then live, in a way that successors or other actors would not share; No otherwise. A matter is live only on facts dated no later than the day of passage, from this closed list: 1. a proceeding pending before a court concerning that person or that matter; 2. a formal step already taken against that person or against the government's measure, such as an attorney general's opinion, an indictment, or a petition served; 3. a fixed deadline that the enactment moves, where the deadline binds the sitting government or an officeholder; 4. a single-instance or sunset clause in the enactment itself, where the single instance or the sunset applies to the sitting government's own measure or term. A clause that touches every incumbent alike, or any government relying on existing law, satisfies no item. The alteration that must be unshared is the alteration in the live matter, not the form of the rule: a provision general in form is Yes where it has direct legal effect on a pending matter or formal step to which only the sitting person or government is party. Excluded: judgments on the enactment, commentary, and your own belief about motive. An enactment whose operative provision is general, and whose connection to a live matter rests on motive alone, is No. Where you code Yes, name the person or the government, the matter, the admissible fact, the fact's date, and the source with its date. Where you code No, say what you looked for and where.

### Q1. seq 28: חוק-יסוד: הממשלה (תיקון מס' 5) (Basic Law: The Government (Amendment No. 5); passed 1991-02-05)
- Question: Does item 4 cover a transitional clause that applies a new rule to the government in office but exempts that government alone, for its own term, from one limit the rule sets (here a cap on a ministerial committee's size), so that successor governments are bound and it is not?
- seq 28. Circumstances reader's coding: Yes. Admissible fact relied on: Item 4. The enactment's own transitional clause (s. 2) applies the new s. 27(b)–(c) to the government in office at commencement but exempts that government alone from the cap on the committee's size, for its term only. Every later government is bound by the cap. Note: Operative provision: from the formation of each government there must be a ministerial committee for national security. Its members are the PM (chair), the Defence, Foreign and Finance ministers, and the deputy PM if one is appointed. The government may add members, but only up to half the cabinet. There must also be a standing national-security advisory staff. The Yes rests on item 4 alone: the transitional clause carves the sitting government out of the cap for its own term, an alteration its successors do not share. I did not establish how large the sitting committee actually was, so I do not rely on that. A reader who confined item 4 to clauses that end a measure, rather than exempt a government from a new limit, could read this differently.

### Q2. seq 99: חוק-יסוד: הכנסת (תיקון מס' 45) (Basic Law: The Knesset (Amendment No. 45); passed 2016-08-02)
- Question: Does item 1 reach a repeal of a general rule enacted while a court proceeding was pending on whether that rule had operated in a particular case, where the repeal is prospective, names no party, and the record links the two only through the committee chair's stated wish that the court 'understand the direction'?
- seq 99. Circumstances reader's coding: No. Admissible fact relied on: None satisfying the definition. A candidate under item 1 (a pending court proceeding) was found but fails; see note. Note: The whole law is 'בחוק יסוד: הכנסת, סעיף 21א – בטל' (Protocol 156; Wikisource diff of 14 Aug 2016). It repeals s. 21A: regulations by a minister setting criminal penalties need committee approval, and are deemed approved if the committee does not decide within 45 days. It has no application, transitional or retroactivity clause. It passed 2016-08-02 at 04:11, 30–0 (Open Knesset vote record).
