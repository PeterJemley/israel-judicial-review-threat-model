# Docket sheet for the comparison sample, redraw, 2026-10-22

You are the docket reader, on the litigation-timing pass. For each enactment in the batch assigned to you, code one variable:

- `litigation_timing`: Yes if litigation bearing on the enactment's subject was pending before the Supreme Court when the enactment passed; No otherwise. Where the enactment was a response to a judgment already given, code No and note the judgment with its date. "Bearing on the subject" means a pending petition or appeal whose outcome the enactment's operative provision would affect, or that concerned the rule the enactment changed; a case that merely cited the Basic Law is not enough.

These enactments are being verified separately as never having been attacked before the Court, and their outcome is attached after that verification. Do not search for challenges and do not record an outcome.
