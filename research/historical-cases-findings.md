# Agent findings: six historical enactments (researched 2026-10-21, not yet applied to the note)

Unverified working notes kept for provenance. Access note: every Knesset website URL redirected to a maintenance page during the search, so Knesset plenum vote records and law PDFs could not be read. Haaretz, Israel Hayom and gov.il election pages returned 403. Where Hebrew Wikipedia, Wikisource, Nevo or Hebrew press were used, the line says so.

## 1. Knesset Elections (Financing) Law 1969, struck in Bergman HCJ 98/69 (3 July 1969), and the post-judgment re-enactment
- Original law enacted 19 Feb 1969 (first reading 29 Jan 1969). Hebrew Wikipedia https://he.wikipedia.org/wiki/חוק_מימון_מפלגות (paraphrase). Versa synopsis https://versa.cardozo.yu.edu/opinions/bergman-v-minister-finance
- Tally: first reading 24 to 2; third reading recorded only as "adopted", no count. Versa PDF https://versa.cardozo.yu.edu/sites/default/files/upload/opinions/Bergman%20v.%20Minister%20of%20Finance.pdf ("The first reading of the Financing Law was passed by the Knesset by a majority of 24 to 2")
- Cross-party private member's bill (Kargman, Nov 1968, signed by members of several factions; opponents only Free Center, HaOlam HaZeh, Maki). Versa: "The Law was initiated by several members of the Knesset as a private bill"
- Re-enactment after the judgment: amended funding bill 76 for, 6 against, 2 abstentions (Hebrew Wikipedia, no date). Versa editor's note: "The amendment was passed by an absolute majority of the Knesset members" and a second law "also by absolute majority, which retroactively confirmed the validity of all legislation concerning election procedures"
- Confirmation of Validity of Laws Law 5729-1969: Sefer HaHukkim 568 p. 204; S.H. 567 is dated 22 Jul 1969, so late July or August 1969, before the 28 Oct 1969 election. Nevo https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law01/190_035.htm . Declaratory and retroactive ("valid for all purposes from their commencement date").
- Expedited: original ordinary; re-enactment within weeks of the judgment and before the election; Court: "the substantive problems raised here require urgent resolution"
- Litigation: original not a response to litigation; re-enactment and Confirmation Law were direct responses to Bergman (Shamgar in Agudat Derekh Eretz https://versa.cardozo.yu.edu/opinions/agudat-derekh-eretz-v-broadcasting-authority ; Shamgar in Mizrahi: "The legislative authority – the Knesset – accepted this Court's ruling in Bergman")

## 2. HCJ 60/77 Ressler v. Chairman of the Central Elections Committee, 31(2) PD 556 (1977). WARNING: sources say this was NOT a party-financing case
- Every source found describes the case as concerning broadcasting-time provisions of the Elections (Modes of Propaganda) Law 5719-1959, which the Court declined to review because the 1969 Confirmation of Validity of Laws Law had been passed by an absolute majority.
- Klinghoffer, 18 Isr. L. Rev. 30 (1983) n. 6: the Court "refused to determine" whether provisions of the Modes of Propaganda Law "encroached upon electoral equality" "on the ground that they were excluded from judicial review by the confirmation of validity Law of 1969". https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/israel-law-review/article/abs/legislative-reaction-to-judicial-decisions-in-public-law/61638D7BEB6509F22F91803174373748
- Landau P. in Agudat Derekh Eretz (Versa): "the Confirmation Law indeed ratified 'the election Laws' mentioned therein by virtue of the absolute majority"
- Barak P. in Mizrahi (Versa PDF): "the Supreme Court has held in the past that a Basic Law may be amended by regular law (HCJ 60/77 Ressler ...)"; panel Etzioni, Y. Cahn, Asher.
- So the enactment "passed by 61 or more" is the 1969 Confirmation Law. Its date, tally and party breakdown: NOT FOUND beyond "absolute majority". Effect: retroactive and declaratory. Enacted in response to Bergman.
- A 1977 Party Financing (Temporary Provisions) Law (published 17 Feb 1977) exists but no source links it to HCJ 60/77. Recommend checking the Hebrew judgment before coding.
- Implication for the note: the "Resler data point" is weaker than stated. The Court did not find an equality violation and uphold anyway; it declined to review because of absolute-majority ratification.

## 3. Basic Law: Human Dignity and Liberty (1992)
- Date 17 Mar 1992. JVL https://www.jewishvirtuallibrary.org/basic-law-human-dignity-and-liberty ; WIPO Lex https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/en/legislation/details/15282
- Tally 32 for, 21 against, 1 abstention. Cheshin J. in Mizrahi (Versa PDF); IDI https://en.idi.org.il/articles/47916 ; Palquest https://www.palquest.org/en/highlight/36118/israel%E2%80%99s-basic-law-human-dignity-and-liberty-1992
- Cross-party: supporters from Likud, Labor, Meretz, Hadash and Yitzhak Levy (NRP). Hebrew Wikipedia https://he.wikipedia.org/wiki/חוק_יסוד:_כבוד_האדם_וחירותו . Sponsors Rubinstein (Shinui) and Justice Minister Meridor (Likud). Ultra-Orthodox bloc opposed. Likud led the government; Labor, Meretz, Hadash in opposition.
- Effect: no commencement clause; published 25 Mar 1992 (S.H. 1391); "effect on publication" is an inference from the absence of a commencement section. Section 10 preserves prior law.
- Process about 11 months (bill April 1991; passed at the end of the 12th Knesset, before the June 1992 election). Ordinary procedure. Entrenchment clause removed by a 27 to 26 vote at second and third reading (Hebrew Wikipedia).
- Litigation: no source indicates enactment in response to or ahead of litigation.

## 4. Basic Law: State Budget for 2017 and 2018 (Special Provisions) (Temporary Provision) 2016, HCJ 8260/16 (6 Sep 2017)
- Date 2 Aug 2016. Hebrew Wikisource https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק-יסוד:_תקציב_המדינה_לשנים_2017_ו-2018_(הוראות_מיוחדות)_(הוראת_שעה) ; Davar https://www.davar1.co.il/27359/
- Tally 50 for, 44 against (Davar, single Hebrew source, paraphrased).
- Party breakdown NOT FOUND; opposition MKs criticised it (JPost 19 Jul 2016 https://www.jpost.com/Israel-News/Politics-And-Diplomacy/Knesset-set-to-approve-two-year-budget-structure-460861).
- Effect: temporary provision, no commencement section, published 11 Aug 2016 (Nevo https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law01/501_445.htm).
- Expedited: roughly two weeks from government bill (about 18 Jul 2016) to final vote, just before summer recess.
- Litigation: enacted after the Court's warning in Bar-On (HCJ 4908/10, 2011); the petition followed enactment (petitioner Academic Center of Law and Business, Ynet https://www.ynet.co.il/articles/0,7340,L-5012934,00.html). Versa HCJ 5658/23 PDF: fifth such temporary provision; Court "issued a nullification notice".

## 5. Basic Law: The Knesset (Amendment No. 50, Temporary Provision) 2020 with indirect amendment of Basic Law: State Economy, HCJ 5969/20 Shafir (23 May 2021)
- Date Monday 24 Aug 2020, vote completed 9:58 p.m., two hours before the dissolution deadline. ToI https://www.timesofisrael.com/knesset-passes-bill-extending-budget-deadline-pushing-off-election-threat/ ; Maariv https://www.maariv.co.il/news/politics/Article-785763
- Tally 67 for, 37 against (ToI: "The bill passed by 67 votes to 37").
- Likud and Blue and White voted for; Yisrael Beytenu opposed; full breakdown NOT FOUND. Ynet https://www.ynetnews.com/article/JK5LX4FYK ; Walla https://www.walla.co.il/news/politics/3382429
- Effect: in force on publication the same day (S.H. 2845, 24 Aug 2020; valid to 23 Dec 2020). Raised the continuation-budget ceiling by 11 billion shekels (Versa HCJ 5658/23 PDF).
- Expedited: 12 days from preliminary reading (12 Aug, 56 to 41) to final vote. ToI https://www.timesofisrael.com/knesset-okays-election-averting-bill-to-delay-budget-in-a-preliminary-reading/ and https://www.timesofisrael.com/bill-to-push-off-budget-deadline-and-avert-elections-approved-for-first-reading/
- Litigation: petitions after enactment (order nisi 24 Nov 2020, Maariv https://www.maariv.co.il/news/law/Article-804191); ruling 6 to 3, funds already disbursed (JPost https://www.jpost.com/israel-news/high-court-sort-of-nixes-basic-law-for-first-time-668909).

## 6. Basic Law: Israel, the Nation State of the Jewish People (2018)
- Date overnight 18 to 19 Jul 2018. Wikisource https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק-יסוד:_ישראל_–_מדינת_הלאום_של_העם_היהודי ; ToI https://www.timesofisrael.com/knesset-votes-contentious-jewish-nation-state-bill-into-law/
- Tally 62 for, 55 against, 2 abstaining (Begin of Likud and Levi-Abekasis). ToI; Haaretz https://www.haaretz.com/israel-news/israel-passes-controversial-nation-state-bill-1.6291048 ; Ynet https://www.ynet.co.il/articles/mobile/0,7340,L-5312834,00.html
- Coalition-only: no source states any opposition MK voted for; two coalition MKs (Amar, Yisrael Beytenu; Hasson, Kulanu) voted against and one abstained. Srugim; ToI https://www.timesofisrael.com/druze-leaders-say-protest-negotiations-over-nation-state-law-to-continue/
- Effect: no commencement section; in force on publication (inference). Publication 26 Jul 2018 (S.H. 2743) from a search summary of a blocked Knesset PDF, unverified. Nevo https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law00/157298.htm
- Process: ordinary multi-year (first proposed 2011 by Dichter, then in opposition), preliminary reading 10 May 2017 (48 to 41), first reading 1 May 2018 (64 to 50), eight-hour final debate with 133 reservations. Not expedited.
- Litigation: about fifteen petitions filed after enactment (IDI https://en.idi.org.il/articles/24241); rejected by an 11-justice panel 8 Jul 2021 (ToI https://www.timesofisrael.com/high-court-rejects-petitions-seeking-to-strike-down-nation-state-law/)
